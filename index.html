FitTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

FitTrack is a fitness and health tracking application designed to help users monitor their weight, body composition, sleep, recovery, and fitness information.

Information We Collect

FitTrack may store information that you choose to enter or connect to the application, including:

  • Weight and weight history
  • Body composition information
  • Target weight and fitness goals
  • Sleep information
  • Recovery information
  • Heart-rate-related information
  • HRV and resting heart rate
  • VO2 Max
  • Workout and strain information
  • Other fitness information provided through supported integrations

When you connect a WHOOP account, FitTrack may request permission to access information from WHOOP, including recovery, cycles, sleep, workouts, profile information, and body measurements.

How We Use Information

Information stored in FitTrack is used to provide features such as:

  • Displaying fitness and health information
  • Tracking changes over time
  • Calculating and displaying fitness statistics
  • Displaying charts and summaries
  • Providing weekly and historical summaries
  • Helping users understand their fitness progress
  • Supporting data export for the user's own analysis

FitTrack does not use WHOOP information for advertising purposes.

WHOOP Integration

If you choose to connect WHOOP to FitTrack, FitTrack will access only the WHOOP information for which you grant permission.

WHOOP authentication is handled through WHOOP's authorization system. FitTrack does not ask you to provide your WHOOP password directly to the application.

You can disconnect the WHOOP connection at any time.

Data Storage

FitTrack is designed to store fitness information for the purpose of providing the application's features.

Data entered manually may be stored locally on your device.

Information obtained through connected services may be stored by FitTrack as necessary to provide synchronization and display features.

Data Sharing

FitTrack does not sell your personal information.

FitTrack does not share your personal information with advertisers.

Information may be transmitted to connected services when required to provide an integration that you have explicitly enabled.

Data Export

FitTrack may provide tools allowing you to export your fitness information in a format suitable for personal analysis.

Data Deletion

You may delete information stored in FitTrack using the application's available data-management features.

You may also disconnect connected services such as WHOOP.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by FitTrack. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

FitTrack is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as FitTrack's features and integrations change.

The updated policy will be published with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or FitTrack's handling of information, please contact the FitTrack developer through the contact information provided with the application.
